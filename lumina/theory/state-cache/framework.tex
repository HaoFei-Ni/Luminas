\begin{abstract}
Working draft for the product method. Numerical losslessness is defined
by the 2-ulp gate; model- and task-level gates live outside this note.
Until Propositions~P1--P3 are proved, call the path a candidate.
\end{abstract}

\section{Scope}
Input: a backbone-agnostic state cache \(S\) (KV, SSM state, or event
state). Output: a code \(C\) and a reconstruction \(\widehat{S}=\mathrm{Dec}(\mathrm{Enc}(S))\).
This note does not prove PPL or task gates.

\section{P0 --- Definition}
\textit{To be written.} Minimal axioms on \(S\): finite, addressable,
independent of a particular backbone. Domains of \(\mathrm{Enc}\) and \(\mathrm{Dec}\).

\section{P1 --- Reconstruction}
\begin{proposition}[P1]
\textit{To be proved.} An error bound for \(\widehat{S}-S\) on the stated domain.
\end{proposition}
\begin{remark}
The current C ABI (\texttt{luma\_kv\_encode\_f32} / \texttt{decode}) is a
finite-checked identity. It occupies the hook; it is not a proof of P1.
\end{remark}

\section{P2 --- Numerical gate}
\begin{proposition}[P2]
\textit{To be closed.} P1 implies the 2-ulp gate:
\(\lvert \widehat{S}_i-S_i\rvert \le 2\cdot 2^{-23}\max(1,\lvert S_i\rvert)\)
for at least \(99.9\%\) of finite elements, and no NaN/Inf.
\end{proposition}
Kernel test: \texttt{lumina/kernel/test/test\_luma\_kv.c}.

\section{P3 --- Downstream equivalence}
\begin{proposition}[P3]
\textit{To be proved.} Conditions under which attention, linear scans, or
spike accumulators treat \(\widehat{S}\) as \(S\).
\end{proposition}
This is the architecture-agnostic closure. Do not claim a law until P3 is proved.

\section{P4 --- Complexity}
\textit{To be written.} Time and \(\lvert C\rvert\) in \(T,H,d\).
Do not report a compression ratio for the identity implementation.

\section{P5 --- Closure and failure}
\textit{To be written.} Boundaries: non-finite inputs, overflow, length or
dynamic-range limits. Align with failed laboratory runs.
